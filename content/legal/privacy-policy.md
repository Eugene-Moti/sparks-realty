# Privacy Policy

**Sparks Properties** ("Sparks Properties", "we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use our website (including any temporary address such as our vercel.app preview), contact us, or use our services, and how we look after it. We follow the **Data Protection Act, 2019 (Kenya)** and its regulations.

By using this website or sending us your details, you confirm that you have read this policy.

## 2. What we collect

**Information you give us**
- **Contact details:** name, phone number, email address, country of residence
- **Enquiry details:** the property you're interested in, your message, budget, preferred viewing date and time, and whether you want an in-person or video viewing
- **Short-stay requests:** check-in and check-out dates, number of guests, special requests
- **Property owners (List With Us):** property address, description, photos, asking price or rent, proof of ownership or authority to let or sell when we take on a mandate
- **Saved searches and newsletter:** your email address and the search filters you choose
- **Conversations** with us by WhatsApp, phone or email

**Collected automatically**
- **Device and usage data:** IP address, browser type, pages viewed, links clicked (for example "WhatsApp" or "Call"), and the site that referred you
- **Cookies and local storage:** see section 9. Favourites, compare lists and recently viewed listings are stored **on your own device**, not on our servers.

**Staff accounts:** for people who log in to our admin panel, we hold their name, email, role and activity logs.

We do **not** knowingly collect sensitive personal data such as health or biometric data, and we do not ask for ID numbers through the website. Any ID or KYC documents needed for a sale or tenancy are collected separately, during the transaction.

| Answering enquiries, arranging viewings and processing short-stay booking requests | Taking steps at your request before a contract, or performing a contract |
| Putting you in touch with the landlord, seller or property owner for a listing you asked about | Taking steps at your request before a contract |

## 4. Who we share it with

We **never sell** your personal data. We share it only with:
- **Landlords, sellers or property owners**, and their appointed agents or lawyers, for the specific property you asked about, and only what they need to arrange a viewing or deal
- **Service providers who process data for us** under written agreements. These are currently:
  - Supabase: database, file storage and logins
  - Vercel: website hosting
  - Our email-delivery provider, which sends enquiry notifications
  - An analytics provider
  - Google/YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook, when you choose to play an embedded video or follow a social link
- **Professional advisers**, such as lawyers, auditors and conveyancers, where needed for a transaction
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it or to protect our rights or someone's safety

## 5. Transfers outside Kenya

Some of our service providers store data on servers outside Kenya (for example in the EU or the USA). When this happens, we make sure appropriate safeguards are in place, as required by sections 48–50 of the Data Protection Act. These include the providers' contractual data-protection terms and security measures. You can ask us for details of these safeguards.

## 7. How we protect it

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls so that only authorised staff can see enquiries, role-based permissions in our admin panel, secure hosting providers, and regular reviews of who has access. No system is completely secure. If a data breach is likely to put you at risk, we will notify the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner within 72 hours, and we will notify you without undue delay, as the law requires.

## 8. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act (sections 26 and 38) you have the right to:
- **Be informed** about how your data is used (this policy)
- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Correct** data that is wrong or incomplete
- **Delete** data that is false, misleading or no longer needed
- **Object** to processing, including direct marketing
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting processing that has already happened
- **Data portability:** receive your data in a commonly used, machine-readable format

To use any of these rights, email **sparkspropertieshomes@gmail.com**. We may need to confirm your identity. We will respond within the time the law requires, and in any case within **7 days** for access requests and **14 days** for correction or deletion requests.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the **Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC)**: www.odpc.go.ke.

## 9. Cookies and similar technologies

- **Essential:** keeps the site working, for example your currency (KES/USD) and theme choice, and admin login sessions. These can't be switched off.
- **Preferences (local storage):** favourites, compare list and recently viewed listings, all kept on your device. You can clear them in your browser at any time.
- **Analytics:** helps us understand which pages and listings are useful. These load only after you accept them in our cookie banner.
- **Embedded content:** YouTube, TikTok and Instagram videos load only when you choose to play them. After that, the platform's own privacy policy applies.

You can change your choice at any time by using the "Cookie settings" link in the footer.

## 10. Children
